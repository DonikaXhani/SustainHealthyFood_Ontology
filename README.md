# SustainHealthyFood Ontology

## Overview

This repository provides the supplementary materials accompanying the research paper:

**Ontology for Personalized Recipe Recommendations Supporting Sustainability, Personal Preferences, and Health Restrictions**

**Authors:** D. Xhani and R. Guizzardi  
**Conference:** 44th International Conference on Conceptual Modeling (ER 2025)  
**Publisher:** Springer Nature Switzerland  
**Pages:** 376–392

The research introduces the **SustainHealthyFood Ontology (SHFO)**, an ontology designed to support personalized recipe recommendations by integrating sustainability considerations, individual food preferences, and health-related dietary restrictions.

The ontology provides a semantic foundation for representing and reasoning about food-related knowledge, supporting the development of recommendation systems that consider multiple dimensions of personalized and sustainable food choices.

### Ontology Availability

## Data Sources

### Nutritional Data — NEVO

The SustainHealthyFood Ontology incorporates nutritional composition data from the **Dutch Food Composition Database (NEVO)**, developed and maintained by the **Rijksinstituut voor Volksgezondheid en Milieu (RIVM)**, the Dutch National Institute for Public Health and the Environment.

The nutritional information integrated into the ontology is derived from **NEVO-online, version 2023/8.0**.

**Data source reference:**

Rijksinstituut voor Volksgezondheid en Milieu (RIVM). (2023). *NEVO online, version 2023/8.0*. RIVM, Bilthoven.  
https://www.rivm.nl/en/dutch-food-composition-database
